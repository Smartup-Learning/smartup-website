# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
Parents and students considering tuition for classes 8–12. Primary geography: Kochi, with online programmes.
## Product Purpose
Marketing and information website. Explain programmes, teaching, centres and provide enquiries. LMS remains a separate app/webapp; integration is deferred by the user.
## Evidence on Hand
Client June 2026 brand PDF, collateral PDF and shared Drive folder. Company established 2014. CBSE and State Board academic operations, verified branch phone/map records, faculty and event photographs. Result announcements, official Smartup YouTube selections, verified iOS/Android destinations and one Kerala Kaumudi article.
## Brand Commitments
Preserve supplied owl/logo variants, Rubik typeface, purple palette and minimal yellow accent. Restrained microanimations/parallax.
## Constraints
Railway-ready website and diagnostic API served by Node 24 with SQLite on a persistent volume. No student accounts, payments or LMS. A private admin password protects the question editor and saved results. Results must remain attributed to their examination/cohort. The user authorised YouTube placeholders and designed sample text testimonials on 11 September 2026. These are prominently labelled and never represented as verified customer quotes. Enquiries use the verified phone and email; the source WhatsApp field is blank.

## Current presentation
The user requested compact horizontal carousels inspired by streaming-service cards. Video stories, photo testimonials, visual media, learning formats and teachers share one pattern. Downloads appear once in the ribbon with platform icons. Life @ Smartup is restored between Team and Centres at the user’s request. Repeated promotional copy stays removed. The team area separates the family group photograph, directors and faculty into three tabs. Synthetic testimonial portraits are visibly disclosed, and real teacher portraits/credentials retain client provenance.

## Smartup Quest
The user requested a separate gamified diagnostic page for Classes 5–10 in four STEM subjects. Every run starts at Foundation, advances after five cumulative correct answers per bucket, carries five lives globally, and ends at zero lives or the enrolled-grade ceiling. The last cleared bucket determines the reported competency. The user approved a draft editable question bank and saving student details/results for the team. An admin editor, JSON import/export, private results table and CSV export support that workflow. The original owl is extruded into real 3D geometry; this extends the supplied brand, not the separate LMS. The draft is explicitly indicative pending educator review. Railway publication follows client approval.

The Quest opens on an uncluttered welcome, followed by details, a dedicated rules step and a continuous question trail. Previously answered questions and explanations resume from the saved attempt. The expressive 3D owl appears beside answer feedback, keeping questions spacious. Lives, subject/current level and bucket progress stay pinned above play; heart-loss animation and level-unlock cards make progression clear. Student details are saved after the displayed data notice, without a guardian checkbox. The homepage diagnostic entry appears only in the hero, alongside the programme action.

Learning Snapshot names the assessed subject, compares current and selected-class levels, provides encouragement based on the gap and a prominent team-guidance action, and summarises quiz performance. The four-subject overview uses one attempt per phone number per subject; unfinished attempts resume and finished scores remain fixed. Access stays in the same browser through an essential credential cookie; number-only and cross-device lookup are not supported.
