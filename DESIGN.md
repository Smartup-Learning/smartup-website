# Smartup design system
Rubik is the client-mandated face. Main purple #5f2ea8, dark purple #392064, cream #f4eee4, ink #1e1e1e, white #ffffff. Yellow #ede60f is limited to small highlights and the main contact action. Display lettering is bold and tightly composed, with a 6rem maximum. Body text is 16px or more.
Large purple and cream areas carry identity. Original PDF-extracted owl and logo are unchanged. Never invert the original logo for white use. The original owl appears beside the hero introduction and moves by at most 11.25px on desktop scroll; it is not a 3D model. Reduced-motion and mobile layouts disable decorative movement.
Marketing flow: hero video cover → attributed results → video stories → photo testimonials → visual media → classes and learning formats → Smartup way → teacher portraits → centres → questions → contact. App downloads live only in the top ribbon with Apple/Android icons. The duplicate Life at Smartup and app-promotion sections are removed.
All five content rails share one carousel pattern: frameless media or simple content cards, a visible next-card edge, manual previous/next buttons, touch scrolling, keyboard Arrow/Home/End navigation, and correct boundary states. No automatic carousel movement. Dark rails use the same spacing and controls as light rails. Videos keep a shared height per breakpoint: 300px desktop, 270px tablet, 260px smaller tablet, 240px narrow mobile. Portraits preserve their narrower proportions; poster crops fill their surface and playback preserves the source video in the player.
Written testimonials use fictional AI portraits with visible sample labels. Actual teacher names and credentials remain sourced from the client. Media includes the original news photograph and a clearly labelled video-coverage placeholder. Text is shortened to one concise introduction or caption where possible; headings avoid forced line breaks outside the main hero.
Interactive controls receive visible keyboard focus. Native HTML contains all marketing information; class and centre choices enhance it with JavaScript. With JavaScript disabled, navigation stays visible, carousels remain horizontally scrollable and video links open YouTube. Motion never gates content.

Media refinement: videos use native YouTube play/pause controls, with no custom close overlays or hero toolbar. Starting another video restores the previous poster. The stories footnote is removed, and the adjoining section padding is 24px on each side. Testimonial photos use a 4:3 aspect ratio with top-centred framing to preserve full faces at every breakpoint.
