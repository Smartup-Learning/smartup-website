# Smartup design system
Rubik is the client-mandated face. Main purple #5f2ea8, dark purple #392064, cream #f4eee4, ink #1e1e1e, white #ffffff. Yellow #ede60f is limited to small highlights and the main contact action. Display lettering is bold and tightly composed, with a 6rem maximum. Body text is 16px or more.
Large purple and cream areas carry identity. Original PDF-extracted owl and logo are unchanged. Never invert the original logo for white use. The small hero owl moves by at most 11.25px on desktop scroll; it is not a 3D model. Reduced-motion and mobile layouts disable decorative movement.
Marketing flow: hero video cover → attributed results → video stories → sample text testimonials → media coverage → class selector and programmes → Smartup way → academic team → community → centres → questions → app downloads → contact. App links also appear in the top bar and hero.
Videos share a standard frame height: 360px desktop, 320px tablet, 340px narrow mobile. Portrait and landscape media retain their proportions. Mobile video cards use horizontal scroll snap; other editorial sections stack. YouTube embeds load only after play, with one active player, close control and external fallback. Thumbnails may load before playback. Quotes remain visibly labelled preview copy.
Interactive controls receive visible keyboard focus. Native HTML contains all marketing information; class and centre choices enhance it with JavaScript. With JavaScript disabled, navigation stays visible and the main phone remains available. Motion never gates content.
