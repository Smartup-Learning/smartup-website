# Smartup design system
Rubik is the client-mandated face. Main purple #5f2ea8, dark purple #392064, cream #f4eee4, ink #1e1e1e, white #ffffff. Yellow #ede60f is limited to small highlights and the main contact action. Display lettering is bold and tightly composed, with a 6rem maximum. Body text is 16px or more.
Large purple and cream areas carry identity. Original PDF-extracted logo assets are unchanged. Never invert the original logo for white use. The header and footer retain the wordmark; the hero owl and introductory eyebrow are removed following the brand consultant’s review. The hero description is “Tuitions • CBSE & State Board • 8 to +2”.
Marketing flow: hero video cover → attributed results → video stories → photo testimonials → visual media → classes and learning formats → Smartup way → team tabs → Life @ Smartup events → centres → questions → contact. App downloads live only in the top ribbon with Apple/Android icons. Life @ Smartup is restored at the client’s request, between Team and Centres. App-promotion remains in the top ribbon only.
The hero footer highlights the user-provided milestones “12+ years”, “10 centres” and “30,000+ Alumni” with yellow numerals and cream labels, beside the retained student-achievements link. Mobile layouts keep all three milestones in one row and put the link below.
All content rails share one carousel pattern: frameless media or simple content cards, a visible next-card edge, manual previous/next buttons, touch scrolling, keyboard Arrow/Home/End navigation, and correct boundary states. No automatic carousel movement. Dark rails use the same spacing and controls as light rails. Videos keep a shared height per breakpoint: 300px desktop, 270px tablet, 260px smaller tablet, 240px narrow mobile. Portraits preserve their narrower proportions; poster crops fill their surface and playback preserves the source video in the player.
Written testimonials use fictional AI portraits with visible sample labels. Actual teacher names and credentials remain sourced from the client. Media includes the original news photograph and a clearly labelled video-coverage placeholder. Text is shortened to one concise introduction or caption where possible; headings avoid forced line breaks outside the main hero.
Section headings lead directly in results, student stories, testimonials, media, programmes, learning formats, Smartup way, teachers and centres. The media heading is “In Media spotlight” The redundant video/testimonial notes beside headings are removed; individual testimonial sample labels remain. FAQ and contact keep their unmarked introductory labels.
Interactive controls receive visible keyboard focus. Native HTML contains all marketing information; class and centre choices enhance it with JavaScript. With JavaScript disabled, navigation stays visible, carousels remain horizontally scrollable and video links open YouTube. Motion never gates content.

Media refinement: videos use native YouTube play/pause controls, with no custom close overlays or hero toolbar. Starting another video restores the previous poster. The stories footnote is removed. Testimonial photos use a 4:3 aspect ratio with top-centred framing to preserve full faces at every breakpoint.

Content sections share 128px top and 96px bottom spacing on desktop, and 88px top and 72px bottom at widths up to 800px. Headings stay close to their content (30px desktop / 25px mobile). The hero uses the shared top inset and a restrained 48px / 40px footer bottom. Opening text is top-aligned. Support for every class is full-width cream, with a white detail panel; Course Offerings remains white, followed by the cream Smartup way. Section headings omit trailing full stops while retaining internal punctuation.

“Course Offerings” switches between CBSE and State Board using accessible tabs. Both panels use the same programme-card and carousel styles. CBSE retains Pro, Trio and Power; State Board has one separately documented online programme, with board-style teaching, SCERT practice, doubt support, assessments and revision. Carousel controls target the selected board and appear only when its cards overflow. Tab controls use the existing purple selection treatment and support arrow keys, Home/End and touch. Without JavaScript, both board programmes remain readable. No CBSE tier names or ratios are assigned to State Board.
Programme sources: client folder `Courses _ Programs/SMARTUP CBSE.pdf`, pages 1, 4 and 7, and `Courses _ Programs/SMART UP ONLINE -STATE (NEW).pdf`, pages 1–4. Exact State Board class durations and parent-meeting schedules are omitted because the source does not establish a consistent promise.

“Meet your Team” has Smartup Family, Directors and Team tabs with the same keyboard behavior as the course tabs. Smartup Family is a dedicated group-photograph panel, as chosen by the user; it currently has a labelled placeholder pending the actual group photo. Directors lists Muhammed Rayees (Chairman), Dhanesh Narayanan (Vice Chairman) and Shihas Muhammed (Director), verified against the company DOCX; initials remain until their supplied portraits can be identified. Team retains the four sourced faculty profiles. Panels and their content remain readable without JavaScript.

Life @ Smartup uses the existing manual carousel and click-to-load video pattern. Three separate official Smartup clips show Talent Eve 4.0, Shoot the Drugs Season 6 and Onam celebrations. They do not repeat the hero or media videos. Deep-purple Team → white Life → cream Centres keeps section boundaries distinct.

Mobile navigation eases down and fades in over 420ms with a gentle, less front-loaded arrival curve, then exits in 280ms; the menu icon follows a 320ms transition. Closed mobile links become inert immediately, visibility hides after the exit, and desktop navigation remains available. Reduced-motion disables all menu transitions. Animation styling is enabled only by JavaScript so the existing no-JS fallback stays visible.

Smartup Quest (`/assessment/`) is a mobile-first game trail within the same brand. A purple welcome stage pairs a dimensional original owl with concise introductory copy and one play action. Separate details and rules steps reduce initial clutter. Subject cards use dimensional graph, magnet, atom and DNA icons in brand colours. Cream question surfaces form a continuous vertical trail on deep purple, with answered questions and expandable explanations retained above the active question. The sticky HUD carries five yellow hearts, subject/current level, stage count and correct-answer progress. Questions use the full card width; the owl appears with answer feedback, smiling on correct answers and gently tilting on incorrect ones. A split-heart effect communicates a lost life, and a purple level-unlock card marks each advancement. Next questions fade upward with an interruptible scroll. Yellow marks actions and earned progress. Rubik, original owl contours, cream and purple materials remain authoritative. Motion is pausable, reduced-motion suppresses animation, sound is opt-in, and a static owl fallback keeps the challenge usable without WebGL. The admin interface remains conventional and unchanged.

The Learning Snapshot uses a spacious report layout instead of the former generic result card: a subject-specific title, a cream current/expected level comparison, and a purple encouragement panel with the owl and a yellow guidance action. Desktop separates quiz performance from the four-subject overview; mobile stacks these with compact paired level labels. A small dimensional subject icon anchors each subject row. No retry action appears for completed subjects. Supportive copy varies between at-grade, one-stage gap and larger gaps.
